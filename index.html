FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can the agent speak Spanish and English?

Yes. It can respond in either language according to the conversation.

Does it book appointments automatically?

The agent can check configured availability and create appointments through the booking system. It does not confirm a booking unless the system succeeds.

Do I need to connect technical integrations myself?

No. Novacode manages the technical channel setup during onboarding. Available channels depend on your configuration.
