About Us

Built for Entrepreneurs,
Powered by AI

NOVACODE TECH LLC is a software company founded in Wyoming by Fernando Perez Belair with a clear mission: make enterprise-grade technology accessible to every entrepreneur in Latin America.

We design and develop AI-powered mobile apps that solve real operational challenges — from inventory management and appointment booking to real estate and event discovery — for the underserved small business market of the Dominican Republic and beyond.

Our approach: deep understanding of local business realities, combined with cutting-edge technology that actually works in the field.
